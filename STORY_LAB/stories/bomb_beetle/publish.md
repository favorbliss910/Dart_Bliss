# The Bomb Beetle: publish kit

Source: Sugiura & Sato (2018), Biology Letters. Japanese toads swallowed bombardier beetles (Pheropsophus jessoensis).
The beetles sprayed about 100 °C chemicals inside the toads, and 43% of the toads vomited them back up, 12-107 minutes later. Most of the beetles walked away alive.
