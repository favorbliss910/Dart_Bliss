## Description
A gold coin for every dead cobra. What could go wrong? 🐍

The story goes that a ruler, desperate to get rid of deadly cobras, paid a reward for every dead snake. At first it worked. Then people realised something: if dead cobras pay, why not BREED them? Secret snake farms popped up. When the reward was cancelled, the breeders released their now-worthless cobras, and there were more snakes than before.

Economists call this the Cobra Effect: when a solution makes the problem worse.

The cobra tale may be a legend, but it really happened with rats. In 1902, French colonial Hanoi paid for every rat tail, and people started cutting off tails, letting the rats go and breeding more.
