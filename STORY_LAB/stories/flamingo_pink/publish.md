## Description
Flamingos aren't born pink. 🦩 They hatch fluffy and grey, and stay that way for their first couple of years.

So where does the pink come from? Their food.

Flamingos eat algae and tiny brine shrimp packed with natural pigments called carotenoids, the same family of pigments that makes carrots orange. Their bodies pull those pigments out and send them into their feathers, skin and legs. The more they eat, the pinker they get.

Take the shrimp away and the colour fades: new feathers grow in pale. That's why zoos add special pigment to their food, otherwise their flamingos would slowly turn white.

Flamingos really are what they eat. 🍤
