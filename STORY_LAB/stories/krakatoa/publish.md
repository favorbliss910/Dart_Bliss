# Krakatoa, the Loudest Sound in History (27 Aug 1883): publish kit

Facts:
- The captain of the British ship Norham Castle, about 40 miles away, wrote that the eardrums of over half his crew were shattered.
- The bang was heard about 3,000 miles away, on Rodrigues Island.
- It was the pressure wave (not audible sound) that circled the globe: barometers recorded it passing up to 7 times, about 3.5 round trips, over 5 days.
- Ash and aerosols made blood-red sunsets worldwide for months.
