## Description
In Iraq, soldiers gathered for a military funeral, complete with a rifle salute. But the one they were burying... was a robot. 🤖

His name was Boomer, a small bomb-disposal robot that took the hits so soldiers didn't have to. And he wasn't the only one. Soldiers named their robots, took them fishing, begged repair shops to "fix Scooby-Doo," and one even ran through machine-gun fire to save his.

Why do humans grieve for machines? And what does it mean when the future of war is full of robots we can't help loving?

⚠️ Some details, like Boomer's funeral and his unofficial medals, come from soldiers' own accounts.

Sources: research by Julie Carpenter (University of Washington), Peter W. Singer's "Wired for War", and reporting by NPR, NBC News and IEEE Spectrum.
