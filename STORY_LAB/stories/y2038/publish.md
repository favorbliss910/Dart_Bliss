## Description
On January 19, 2038, at 03:14:07 UTC, some computers will think it's 1901. ⏰💥

Many systems count time as the number of seconds since January 1, 1970. Older ones store that count in a signed 32-bit number, which tops out at 2,147,483,647. One second later, it overflows, and the clock jumps back to December 13, 1901.

Most modern phones and computers already use 64-bit time, which won't run out for about 292 billion years, roughly 20 times the age of the universe. The danger is the old stuff nobody updates: embedded systems, industrial machines, cars and forgotten servers still quietly counting toward the limit.

It's the Y2K sequel. Will we be ready? 👇
