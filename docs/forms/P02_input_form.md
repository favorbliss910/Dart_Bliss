## Budget note (read before running)

The script is about 330 words. At `am_michael` speed 1.0 with the pauses it needs, that is roughly
2 to 2.5 minutes of narration, or about 3,000 to 3,600 frames at 24 fps.

At the measured P01 rate (about 31 s per frame at 540×960 on this 4-CPU cloud container), that is
**about 26 to 31 hours of rendering for the proof resolution alone**, roughly 10 times the
`MAX_RENDER_HOURS: 3` in the form. The master prompt will stop at Stage 6 and ask. Ways to bring it in:

1. **Render a pilot first**: shots 1, 8, 20 and 22 (about 25 s, roughly 5 h). This proves the
   hardest looks: vanishing, the water outline and the reveal.
2. **Lower proof settings**: 16 samples at 360×640 cuts the cost to about a third (about 9 to 10 h for everything).
3. **GPU compute**: one modern NVIDIA GPU is typically 10 to 30 times faster (not verified in this repo).
4. **Raise MAX_RENDER_HOURS** and run it in batches of a few shots per session, since the renderer is restart-safe.

The new looks this film needs that P01 never proved (they must pass the Stage 5 lookdev gate):
the vanish shimmer, the floating hologram, an interior lighting recipe and the water-outline shader.
