There's a library in Norway where every book is blank, and people wait years to get inside. The building has no windows, no signs, and only one librarian who never speaks. You pick a shelf, you pick a book, and you sit down to read nothing. But here's the strange part. Everyone who walks out swears the pages told them a story. Their story, with names and places nobody else could know. One man left crying because the book described the night his father disappeared, down to the color of the car. So scientists hid a camera. For three hours, the pages stayed completely empty. When they played the footage back, the librarian was standing behind every reader, whispering. So, be honest. Would you open the book?
